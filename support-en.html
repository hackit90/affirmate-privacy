Get in touch, without your journal

For app, privacy or accessibility questions, email support@swisswise.com. The link opens your email app; no message is sent until you choose to send it. You may also use the publisher's existing contact page, which is a separate website.

Tell us your AffirMate version, iOS version, the feature involved, and what you expected to happen. Include a screenshot only if you want to, and remove personal text, names and images first. Never send journal entries, passwords, Apple account credentials, full payment details or device backups.

We receive your email address and whatever you voluntarily send, and use that information to handle your request. The app does not attach your local content automatically. For questions about support correspondence or your privacy rights, use the same address. We cannot access or recover the writing stored on your device.

Where your words are kept

Your optional name, preferences, favorites, personal affirmations, journal entries, drafts, moods and imported background are stored locally. The app and its widget use a local App Group container. Reading and the included instrumental music do not require an AffirMate account or a cloud service.

Local storage is not a promise that no copy can leave the phone: your iOS or iCloud device backup may include app data. AffirMate has no own cloud synchronization. Manage your Apple backups separately; we do not hold a server copy of your journal.

Export before you reset

In Profile, choose Export your words to save favorites, personal words, journal reflections and your current draft as a readable text file. Export is available when there is writing to export. Choose the destination in the iOS file picker.

This is a writing export, not a complete, restorable app backup: it does not promise to include moods, pictures or every setting, and it cannot be imported to rebuild the app. The selected storage location or sharing service handles the copy under its own rules.

Delete or start fresh

You can delete individual journal entries and personal affirmations in the app. Profile also offers Reset personal data with a confirmation step. Reset removes personal writing, moods, reminders and customization, including the imported background copy. Export anything you want to keep first.

Reset does not remove Apple account purchases, images already saved to Photos, exported or shared files, or older device backups. Remove those copies separately if you need to. Removing an imported background does not delete its original from your photo library.

Choose what appears outside the app

Widgets use included daily thoughts by default. If you select favorites or personal words as the widget source, those words can be seen by anyone who can see your Home or Lock Screen. Journal and mood entries are not widget content.

Reminders are optional, local notifications with no private writing. Turn them off in AffirMate or iOS Settings. Declining photo or notification permissions does not prevent reading.

Apple purchases and trial access

Where purchases are enabled in your version, Apple handles payment and AffirMate checks verified StoreKit transactions on your device. We do not process card or bank details, and no AffirMate account is required. A trial does not automatically turn into a paid purchase.

Purchasing and trial availability depend on the released app version and Apple's product availability. This page does not offer a purchase, promise an available trial or quote a price. Only an explicit purchase confirmed through Apple can start a paid unlock.

If your version offers Restore Purchases, use that control with the Apple account used for the original purchase. Restoring a trial keeps its original end date. If purchases are disabled, restore does not change your access. For billing, purchase history or refund requests, use Apple's official support.

A practice, not a treatment

Affirmations, moods and instrumental music are for personal reflection. They do not diagnose or treat conditions and do not replace medical or psychological care. Reading requires no name, photo permission or reminders.

If an accessibility barrier prevents you from using a feature, tell us which screen and which accessibility setting is involved. Please share only the technical details you are comfortable sending.

About this website

These static pages contain no scripts, analytics, forms, remote fonts or third-party assets, and do not set cookies themselves. Website hosting is separate from the app: when these pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub receives technical web requests and logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. Its privacy statement explains its processing, retention and international transfers. This does not send your local AffirMate journal, moods or pictures to GitHub. Opening a link to Apple or Swisswise takes you to a different website with its own data handling.
